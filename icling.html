About dry mouth

About 1 in 5 people live with dry mouth

Saliva cleans the mouth, helps with speaking and swallowing, and protects the teeth. When the glands make too little of it, the mouth feels dry and sticky, and the risk of tooth decay, gum disease and infection goes up.

Rates are much higher among people treated for cancer with radiation or chemotherapy, and among people taking certain everyday medicines.

Common signs

Dry or sticky mouthConstant thirstHard to swallow, speak or chewBad breathBurning tongueCracked lipsMore tooth decay

Dry mouth that lasts should be checked by a doctor or dentist.

Questions

Frequently asked

Can I use iCling™ during radiation or chemotherapy?
Yes. iCling™ was developed with people who have dry mouth from cancer treatment in mind and can be used during active treatment. Ask your care team if you are unsure.
Is it suitable for people with diabetes?
Yes. It is sugar-free and does not affect blood glucose levels.
Does it interact with other medicines?
iCling™ is not swallowed as a medicine and does not affect how other medicines are absorbed.
What if I swallow the film by accident?
No need to worry. It breaks down naturally. The relief will simply end sooner.
Can I use it while driving or working?
Yes. The film is discreet and does not get in the way of talking, driving or sipping a drink.
Has it been tested?
In initial studies with cancer patients and older adults with chronic dry mouth, 91% of users gave positive feedback. Users reported faster relief, about 90 minutes of effect on average, and better sleep and speech.
