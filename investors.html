Why Pharmedica

Six reasons in plain words

A platform, not one product

One production technology serves many medicines, supplements and conditions. Each new partner product reuses the same core.

Proven medicines, new format

Partners reformulate drugs that already work. That is a faster route to market than developing a new drug from scratch.

Beyond what films could do

Up to 150 mg per film, release from minutes to 12 hours, and several ingredients at once. Other film makers stop at about 50 mg and minutes.

Already selling

iCling™ for dry mouth launched in Q4 2025, with production at scale and Israel's largest dental chain, Maccabi-Dent, as a strategic shareholder.

Protected in key markets

Granted patents in the US and Europe, with further US applications on the manufacturing process.

Experienced leadership

Founders with decades in drug delivery, engineering and medtech; a chairman who ran Yeda, the Weizmann Institute's commercial arm.

Business model

How Pharmedica earns

Business-to-business: pharma partners pay for development, then share revenue on every product sold. Pharmedica's own products, starting with iCling™, add direct sales.

1 · Partner

Selects existing drugs

From its own approved portfolio.

2 · Pharmedica

Custom development and manufacturing

Paid as one-time development fees.

3 · Market

Partner sells the P/ODF product

Pharmedica receives a share of the revenue.

Phase 1 · Q1 2026 – Q4 2027

Prove the technology

Launch three products to validate the platform and its real-world performance. Global sales through distribution partners.

Phase 2 · Q1 2027 – Q4 2029

Scale through pharma

Strategic partnerships with leading pharma companies to expand their existing product lines with P/ODF.
